Your notebook, your choice

On your phone.
With a backup when you want one.

Your notebook is stored on your device. Backup, sync, and sharing are optional, and Android system backup follows your device settings.

Connect Google Drive when you want to back up or restore your notebook. D Notes Pro adds automatic sync. You decide whether to connect.

The app uses Firebase Analytics and crash reporting to help improve reliability.
