Damian Local Google Workspace

A personal integration operated by Damian van den Berg. It uses the Google Workspace CLI on his computer to read and manage his Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Tasks when he requests assistance from a local AI agent. It is not a public service or an officially supported Google product.

Gmail access supports scoped searches, drafts, labels and sending approved messages. Calendar and Tasks access supports scheduling and task management. Each Google account must explicitly authorize access. The integration does not make email, calendars or tasks public.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026.

The integration accesses account identity, email and related metadata, calendars and events, and task lists and tasks as needed for the requested work. It does not automatically copy the full mailbox or maintain a public collection of Google data.

OAuth credentials are stored outside the source repository on the local computer. Refresh credentials are encrypted, with the encryption key stored in the operating system keyring. Temporary local caches and task output may be created during use.

When an AI agent is used, selected Google content and operation results may be sent to the AI provider for processing and may be retained in conversation history under that provider's terms and privacy policy. A local command does not mean all processing happens offline. Information is sent to an email recipient or another destination only as part of an authorized action.

Google data is used to provide the requested personal productivity functionality. It is not sold or used for advertising. Damian Local Google Workspace's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Local authentication can be removed with the CLI logout command. Provider conversation history and files must be managed separately in the relevant application; revocation does not erase those copies.

This integration is for its owner's personal use. Privacy questions can be directed to Damian through the contact options on his personal website.

Terms of use

This integration is for Damian's personal use and is not offered as a public service. It depends on Google's APIs, the Google Workspace CLI and the selected AI client. Access remains subject to their applicable policies and terms. Generated drafts and proposed changes require review under the owner's configured approval rules.
